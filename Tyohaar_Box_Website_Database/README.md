# Tyohaar Box — Website Product Knowledge Base

Prepared for use in Google Antigravity or Google AI Studio website projects.

## What is included
- `data/products.json`: 5 named ready-made hampers and 1 customized-hampers catalogue entry.
- `data/brand.json`: brand details, location, phone numbers, WhatsApp, Instagram.
- `data/products.csv`: simple product listing for import/reference.
- `images/original_photos/`: unchanged copies of the camera photographs.
- `images/promotional_posters/`: unchanged copies of the provided promotional artwork.

## Website-builder instructions
1. Read `data/products.json` and `data/brand.json`; use these as the source of truth.
2. Build sections for Ready-made Hampers and Customized Hampers.
3. Show the `main_image` on each product card and `gallery` in its detail page. Resolve paths relative to this folder's root, or move them into the website public directory and update paths consistently.
4. Use image rendering with `object-fit: contain` and preserve original aspect ratio. Do not stretch, crop, redraw, or replace the product photographs.
5. Do not display or invent prices, discounts, stock levels, delivery promises, additional items, quantities, or product specifications.
6. Use WhatsApp +91 94179 08933 for enquiries and show the second contact number +91 76966 81584, location Patiala, Punjab, and Instagram @tyohaarbox.in.
7. The customized hamper is made on special request. Its three-potli photo is an EXAMPLE, not a fixed product specification.
8. Premium Treats has two owner-confirmed camera photographs showing different arrangements; its published contents come from the poster. Do not imply all pictured variations are identical.
9. Little Joy Box and Festive Treats have promotional posters only, no camera photographs supplied.
10. Do not infer product contents from partially obscured packages. No price fields are included.

## Image quality
All image files are byte-for-byte copies of the user's uploads. No enhancement has been applied yet, to avoid changing packaging or labels. Enhanced versions can be added later after user review while retaining these originals.
